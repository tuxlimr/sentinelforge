# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in SentinelForge to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Keep security discussions responsible: report vulnerabilities privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Using this project to attack systems you are not authorized to test.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider harmful.

Report problems by contacting the maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel (the **Security**
tab, **Report a vulnerability**) and marking the report as a conduct concern. Reports are handled confidentially.
